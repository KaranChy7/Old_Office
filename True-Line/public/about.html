Who We Are

Melbourne-based. Precision-driven. Work we stand behind.

True-Line Installs brings proper trade equipment and a sharp eye for detail to every custom metal fabrication and installation job across Melbourne. From wrought iron gates to architectural cladding, every project gets the same attention — showing up on time, communicating clearly, and never cutting corners just to move on to the next one.

From full architectural fit-outs to one-off custom pieces, every job gets the same trade-standard care — whether it's a single grille or a full building facade. We work across Carrum Downs and greater Melbourne, serving Frankston, the Mornington Peninsula, Dandenong and beyond.
