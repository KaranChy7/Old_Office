Who We Are

This website is operated by True-Line Installs, 6/43 Yazaki Way, Carrum Downs VIC 3201. We respect your privacy and handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

What We Collect

When you send an enquiry or request a quote, we collect the details you provide, which may include:

  • your name, phone number and email address
  • your suburb or project location
  • details, timeline and budget for your project

How We Use It

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry, prepare quotes, and carry out and follow up on work you engage us for. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Who We Share It With

We only share information where it's needed to deliver your job — for example with a supplier or subcontractor working on your project — or where the law requires it.

Storage & Security

Enquiries are delivered to our business email. We take reasonable steps to protect your information from misuse, loss and unauthorised access, and delete it when it's no longer needed.

Access & Corrections

You can ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, at any time.
