about us

who we are

With over 23 years of proven industry experience, Nasiri Building & Renovation Services has grown into a trusted name in construction and renovations across NSW. Based in Guildford, we proudly service clients within an 80km radius, offering dependable solutions backed by quality workmanship and strong customer relationships. Our reputation is built on consistency, reliability, and the ability to bring our clients’ visions to life.

We specialise in a wide range of residential and commercial building services, including new builds, structural construction, landscaping, pool building, and home additions. Whether it’s a modern family home, a functional commercial space, or a stylish renovation, we approach every project with dedication and attention to detail. No job is too big or too small—we treat every project with the same level of care and professionalism.
