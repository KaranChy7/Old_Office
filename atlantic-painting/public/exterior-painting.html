Thorough Preparation Before Painting

A new coating needs a sound, suitably prepared surface. Applying paint over loose coatings or unresolved damage can affect the finished result.

Depending on the surface and agreed scope, preparation may include:

  • Cleaning away dirt, residue and surface contaminants.
  • Scraping loose or peeling paint.
  • Sanding rough edges and weathered areas.
  • Filling minor surface imperfections.
  • Treating suitable areas of surface rust.
  • Applying primers matched to the material and coating system.
  • Protecting windows, paths, plants and nearby fixtures.

Rot, ongoing water entry or significant building damage may need repairs before painting can proceed.

Coatings Selected for Outdoor Conditions

Different exterior materials require different products. We assess the substrate and existing finish before discussing suitable primers and topcoats.

Colour, sheen, exposure and maintenance needs are considered as part of the selection. We follow the chosen coating system’s application requirements, including suitable weather conditions and drying intervals.

A Colour Scheme That Complements Your Property

Exterior colours can bring walls, roofing, trims and entrances together. We discuss your preferred look and how it relates to existing features such as brickwork, paving and landscaping.

Whether you prefer a subtle refresh or a more noticeable change, considering the full exterior helps create a cohesive result. Sample colours can also help you assess their appearance in natural light.

Weather and Access Planning

Exterior painting depends on suitable conditions. Rain, excessive heat, humidity and surface moisture can affect application and drying, so scheduling may need to change with the weather.

We also discuss access around the building and identify whether specialist equipment is required. These requirements are considered when preparing the project scope and quote.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know when my exterior needs repainting?

Fading, peeling, cracking and worn coatings can indicate that the surface needs attention. An assessment helps identify the preparation or repairs required.

Can you paint over existing exterior paint?

This depends on its condition and compatibility with the proposed coating. Loose or unsuitable paint needs to be addressed before repainting.

Can you paint brick or rendered walls?

We can assess these surfaces and discuss suitable options. Moisture, existing coatings and the condition of the material need to be considered first.

What happens if it rains during the project?

Work may be rescheduled to protect the preparation and finish. Painting resumes when conditions and surfaces are suitable for the selected products.

How long will exterior painting take?

Timing depends on the property’s size, surface condition, access, preparation and weather. We discuss an estimated schedule after assessment.

Will painting fix leaks or damaged timber?

Leaks, rot and other underlying defects require appropriate repairs. We discuss visible issues so they can be addressed before the affected surfaces are coated.
