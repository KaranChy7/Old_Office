about us
Your Comfort Is Our Priority

21 Degrees ACR is a business with highly experienced technicians operating in Perth WA. We've built our reputation on quality workmanship, honest service, and getting the job done right the first time, for homeowners and businesses throughout the local area.

From domestic split systems to commercial and ducted air conditioning, cool rooms, freezer rooms and refrigeration, we bring the same attention to detail to every job. No shortcuts, no rushed work—just reliable, professional service you can depend on.

Every property has different heating, cooling and refrigeration needs, and we approach each job with the skill and adaptability to match. Whether it's installing a domestic split system, servicing a commercial unit, maintaining a cool room, or safely decommissioning old equipment, our range of services means you can rely on one experienced team for the job.

We believe a great experience matters just as much as a great result. That's why we prioritise clear communication, punctual service, and tidy, respectful work on every site we visit. Our clients appreciate our professionalism as much as our workmanship, making the whole process smooth and stress-free.

As a locally operated business servicing Ellenbrook and the surrounding area, we're proud to support our community and maintain a standard of service that keeps clients coming back. At 21 Degrees ACR, we deliver more than an installation or repair—we deliver comfort, reliability, and results that last.
