About us

Saqib Transport Pty Ltd is a trusted name in the transport and removals industry, proudly serving Sydney and surrounding suburbs for over 10 years. We are committed to delivering reliable, safe, and professional services tailored to your needs, whether it’s a local move, commercial relocation, or courier delivery. Our experienced team takes great care to handle every job with efficiency and respect, ensuring your belongings and goods arrive safely, on time, every time.

With a strong focus on customer satisfaction and competitive pricing, Saqib Transport offers a wide range of services, including truck and trailer hire, furniture removals, residential and commercial moves, and office relocations. Backed by a modern, well-maintained fleet and fully insured services, we guarantee peace of mind and a stress-free experience from start to finish.

Our core values guide everything we do, delivering dependable, high-quality, and customer-centered service.
