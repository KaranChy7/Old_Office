Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Painting questions are always welcome — we'd rather you understand the full picture before we start. Here are the most common things people ask us.

How long does a typical painting job take?
+
Project timelines depend on the size and complexity of the work. A single room interior can be completed in a day, while a full exterior repaint might take three to five days. We'll provide a realistic timeline in your quote and keep you updated throughout.
Do I need to move my furniture before you arrive?
+
We recommend clearing light items from the walls and breakables from surfaces. Large furniture can usually be moved by us to the centre of the room and covered with drop sheets. Just let us know and we'll advise what preparation is needed for your specific job.
What brands and types of paint do you use?
+
We work primarily with premium Australian brands such as Dulux, Taubmans, and Haymes — using the product that best suits the surface, environment, and finish required. For exterior work in Queensland, we always use weatherproof, UV-stable formulations as standard.
Do you service areas outside of Inala?
+
Absolutely. We service all suburbs within approximately 50km of our Inala base, which covers a wide range of Brisbane south-west, western, and surrounding communities. If you're unsure whether we cover your area, just give us a call at 0409 904 617 — we're happy to confirm.
Is your work guaranteed?
+
Yes. We stand behind every job we complete. If any aspect of our workmanship causes concern within a reasonable period after completion, we'll return to assess and rectify it at no extra cost. Your satisfaction is the measure of our success.
How do I get a quote? Is it really free?
+
Completely free and with no obligation. Simply call us, email us, or fill in the form on this page, and we'll arrange a convenient time to view your project. We'll provide a written quote outlining all costs before any work begins.
Can you help with colour selection?
+
We love helping clients with colour choices — it's one of the most impactful decisions of any repaint. Drawing on 26 years of experience, we can suggest palettes that suit your home's architecture, natural light, and personal style — and we always recommend testing a sample patch before full application.
