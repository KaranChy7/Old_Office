About Us

At Parkinson Roofing, we’re more than just roofing experts—we’re a dedicated team that puts your satisfaction first.

With a strong focus on reliable service, skilled workmanship, and clear communication, we go above and beyond to deliver quality results. Whether it’s a small repair or a full roof restoration, our goal is to exceed expectations and leave you with a roof you can trust.

Durability You Deserve!

At Parkinson Roofing, we take pride in offering expert roofing services across Campbelltown and its neighboring regions, providing trustworthy solutions supported by high-quality craftsmanship and extensive industry experience.

Our experienced team is focused on safeguarding homes and businesses with resilient roofing services customized to meet the specific requirements of each property.

We provide comprehensive roofing solutions, Roof Repairs, Roof Maintenance, Roof Restoration, Rebedding / Ridge Repair, General Maintenance, Bird Proofing, colorbond replacement/ roof plumbing, utilizing top-grade materials and established techniques.

At Parkinson Roofing, communication and transparency are central to our approach. From the initial consultation to project completion, we work closely with our clients to understand their needs and expectations, offering expert advice and clear timelines. Our 24-hour availability from Monday to Saturday means you can count on us whenever roofing issues arise, minimizing disruptions and protecting your investment.

We take immense pride in the trust our clients place in us and strive to exceed expectations on every job. Parkinson Roofing is committed to delivering reliable, cost-effective roofing solutions that stand the test of time, backed by professionalism and a genuine passion for excellence.
