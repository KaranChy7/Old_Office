Strong Fences Built for Rural Queensland

Colls Rural Contractors

Based in Collinsville and led by director Clay Peter Colls, we are a hands-on fencing contractor servicing properties up to 300 km from our base. With 5 years of experience in rural and commercial fencing, we build fences that stand up to tough North Queensland conditions — from long boundary runs and stock yards to Colorbond fencing for homes and businesses.

We handle every job from start to finish, including site assessment, line marking, post installation, wire straining, panel fitting and clean-up. Whether you need a new fence on a cattle property, a secure perimeter for a commercial site or repairs after storm or stock damage, we deliver solid workmanship and straight, well-strained fence lines that last.

Reliable Fencing Solutions Built to Last

We provide dependable rural, commercial and Colorbond fencing tailored to your property, with a strong focus on durability, quality materials and straight, well-built fence lines. Servicing Collinsville and surrounds up to 300 km, 7 days a week.

  • Colorbond panel repairs
  • Storm and flood damage repairs
