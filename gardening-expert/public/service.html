Our Fencing Services:

Rural Contract Fencing

A good fence is one of the most important assets on any rural property. It keeps stock where they belong, marks your boundaries and protects your land, crops and livelihood. At Colls Rural Contractors, we provide contract fencing for farms, cattle stations and lifestyle blocks across Collinsville and surrounding areas, travelling up to 300 km to get the job done.

We build boundary fences, paddock subdivisions, laneways and stock yards to suit the way you run your property. Every job starts with a proper look at the site — the terrain, soil type, creek crossings and the type of stock you're running — so we can recommend the right posts, wire and layout for long-term performance.

North Queensland conditions are tough on fences. Hard ground, heavy wet seasons, floods and cattle pressure all take their toll. That's why we use quality materials, solid strainer assemblies and correct wire tension to build fence lines that stay straight, tight and secure for years to come.

We work with steel posts, timber strainers and a range of wire options including barbed, plain and hinge-joint, choosing the combination that best suits your stock and budget. Long runs are carefully lined and marked so the finished fence looks as good as it performs.

We understand that time on the land is precious. Our team works 7 days a week from 6:00 AM to 6:00 PM and we plan every job to minimise disruption to your daily operations and stock movements.

With 5 years of experience in rural fencing, we take pride in delivering honest quotes, reliable service and workmanship you can count on. When you need fencing done right on your property, Colls Rural Contractors is ready to help.

Commercial Rural Fencing

Commercial rural sites need fencing that is strong, secure and built to handle large scale operations. At Colls Rural Contractors, we deliver commercial rural fencing for agricultural businesses, grazing operations, mine sites, councils and industrial yards throughout the Collinsville region and beyond.

From long-distance boundary fencing and large stock yards to perimeter fencing around infrastructure, we have the equipment and know-how to complete bigger projects efficiently. We work closely with property managers and site supervisors to understand project requirements, timelines and access conditions before work begins.

Safety and reliability are a priority on every commercial job. We plan our work carefully, follow site requirements and keep clear communication with your team from start to finish so the project runs smoothly and on schedule.

Our commercial fencing solutions are tailored to each site, whether you need heavy-duty stock fencing, security fencing for equipment and machinery, or fencing to separate work zones. We source quality materials designed to withstand heavy use and harsh conditions.

With a service radius of up to 300 km, we are well placed to support commercial clients in remote and regional areas where reliable contractors can be hard to find.

At Colls Rural Contractors, we bring the same care and attention to detail to commercial projects as we do to every rural job — delivering durable fencing that protects your assets and supports your operations for years to come.

Colorbond Fencing

Colorbond fencing is one of the most popular fencing choices in Australia, and for good reason. It's strong, low maintenance, termite proof and looks great on homes, businesses and rural properties alike. At Colls Rural Contractors, we supply and install quality Colorbond fencing across Collinsville and surrounding areas.

Colorbond fencing provides excellent privacy and security, and it holds up well in the Queensland climate. It won't rot, warp or need painting, making it a smart long-term investment for any property owner looking for a clean, modern finish.

We install Colorbond fencing for backyard boundaries, pool surrounds, side fences, commercial perimeters and house yards on rural blocks. A range of colours is available so you can choose a finish that suits your property.

Correct installation is the key to a Colorbond fence that lasts. We set posts at the right depth and spacing, ensure panels are level and straight, and follow the slope of your land for a neat, professional result.

We can also install matching gates, retaining plinths and extensions to complete your fence and make it practical for everyday use.

From the first quote to the final clean-up, we make the process simple and hassle-free. With Colls Rural Contractors, you get a quality Colorbond fence installed properly the first time.

Wire Fencing

Wire fencing remains the backbone of rural Australia, providing a cost-effective and reliable way to contain stock and fence large areas. At Colls Rural Contractors, we install all types of wire fencing to suit cattle, horses, sheep, goats and general boundary requirements.

Our wire fencing options include barbed wire, plain wire, hinge-joint, ringlock, mesh and combination fences. We'll help you choose the right setup for your stock, terrain and budget, so you get a fence that does the job without overspending.

The strength of a wire fence comes down to its strainers and tension. We build solid end and corner assemblies and strain every line correctly, giving you a tight, straight fence that holds up against stock pressure and the weather.

We also install wire fencing for lifestyle blocks, house yards, dog enclosures and garden areas, where a practical and affordable fence is needed.

Whether it's a short run or several kilometres of fencing, we approach every job with the same care, planning the line properly and working efficiently to deliver a quality result.

Backed by 5 years of experience, Colls Rural Contractors delivers wire fencing that is built to last and suited to the demands of North Queensland properties.

Fence Repairs

Fences take a beating from storms, floods, fallen trees, vehicles and stock. A damaged fence can quickly lead to lost stock, security problems and bigger costs down the track. At Colls Rural Contractors, we provide fast and reliable fence repairs for rural, commercial and residential properties.

We repair all types of fencing, including barbed and plain wire, hinge-joint and mesh fences, Colorbond panels and gates. Common repairs include re-straining sagging wire, replacing broken or rotted posts, rebuilding strainer assemblies and fixing flood-damaged sections.

Every repair starts with an honest assessment. We'll tell you whether a section can be repaired or whether replacement is the better long-term option, so you can make an informed decision that suits your property and budget.

After the wet season or a major storm, getting your fences back up quickly is critical. We work 7 days a week and travel up to 300 km, so we can get to your property and restore your fence lines as soon as possible.

We use quality materials that match or improve on your existing fence, making sure repaired sections are strong, tight and blend in with the rest of the line.

Whatever the damage, Colls Rural Contractors is here to help. Contact us today to arrange a fence repair and get your property secure again.

Mesh Fencing

Mesh fencing is a versatile option for rural, agricultural and commercial properties where dependable containment and clear boundary protection are required. Colls Rural Contractors can install mesh fencing for a range of applications while keeping strength, practicality and long-term performance in mind.

This type of fencing can be suitable for livestock areas, paddocks, smaller animals, property boundaries and sections where a more enclosed barrier is preferred. Different mesh styles can be selected depending on the intended use and the conditions of the property.

Correct tensioning and secure attachment are essential when installing mesh fencing. We carefully position posts and fencing materials to create a consistent fence line that remains stable and performs effectively once the project is completed.

Mesh fencing can also work well across large rural areas because it provides visibility while still creating a reliable physical barrier. It can be incorporated with gates and other fencing styles to suit different sections of the same property.

Where existing mesh fencing has become loose, damaged or worn, sections can also be assessed for repair or replacement. Addressing damaged areas early can help maintain the security of livestock and reduce the chance of further deterioration.

Colls Rural Contractors provides straightforward mesh fencing solutions designed around the needs of rural properties. Our goal is to complete each installation neatly, securely and with careful consideration of how the fence will be used.

Barbed Wire Fencing

Barbed wire fencing is commonly used across rural and agricultural properties where strong livestock control and clearly defined boundaries are required. Colls Rural Contractors provides barbed wire fencing solutions for farms, paddocks, acreage properties and larger rural areas.

A properly installed barbed wire fence relies on strong posts, correct wire spacing and consistent tension. We focus on each of these elements to help create a secure fence line suitable for demanding rural environments.

Barbed wire can be installed as a complete fencing system or combined with other fencing materials depending on the property and livestock requirements. The number of strands and overall fence configuration can also be planned according to the intended purpose.

This fencing option is particularly useful for long boundary lines because it provides an effective and practical solution without making the property feel completely enclosed. It is commonly used for cattle areas, grazing land and general rural boundaries.

Older barbed wire fences may eventually experience loose wires, damaged posts or broken sections. We can assist with identifying problem areas and carrying out repairs or replacements where required to restore the strength of the fence.

At Colls Rural Contractors, we aim to provide barbed wire fencing that is practical, well-installed and suited to everyday rural use. Each job is approached with careful planning and attention to the conditions of the property.

Cattle Rail Assemblies

Cattle rail fencing provides a strong and practical solution for areas where livestock need to be safely contained and managed. Colls Rural Contractors can install cattle rail assemblies for yards, holding areas, gateways and other sections of rural properties where additional strength is required.

Cattle areas often experience greater pressure than standard property boundaries, which means the fencing needs to be constructed with durability in mind. Secure posts and properly installed rails help create a stable structure that can handle regular livestock movement.

We can plan cattle rail assemblies around the layout and operational requirements of the property. This may include connecting rails with existing fencing, incorporating gates or creating dedicated sections for handling and controlling livestock.

Good access is especially important around cattle yards and working areas. We consider the positioning of entrances and fence lines so that livestock, vehicles and equipment can move through the area as efficiently as possible.

Cattle rail fencing can also provide a clean and organised appearance while maintaining the heavy-duty characteristics required for rural use. Quality installation helps reduce unnecessary movement and improves the overall integrity of the structure.

Colls Rural Contractors provides practical cattle rail solutions for farmers and rural property owners throughout Collinsville and surrounding areas. Our approach focuses on strong workmanship, sensible layouts and reliable finished results.

Front Entrance

A well-designed front entrance creates a strong first impression while providing practical access to a rural property. Colls Rural Contractors can construct and install front entrance fencing and gate areas that complement the property while maintaining durability and functionality.

Rural entrances need to accommodate everyday traffic, including cars, utes, machinery, trailers and other equipment. For this reason, the width, positioning and overall layout of the entrance should be planned carefully before installation begins.

We can integrate entrance fencing with rural wire fencing, mesh fencing, cattle rails or other existing boundary systems. This creates a more consistent appearance while helping ensure the entrance remains secure and easy to use.

Strong posts and properly supported gates are important for maintaining the reliability of a front entrance over time. We focus on secure installation and suitable positioning to help reduce unnecessary movement, sagging or alignment problems.

The appearance of the entrance can also be considered as part of the project. A neat, professionally installed entryway can improve the presentation of a farm, acreage property or rural business while clearly identifying the main access point.

Whether you are establishing a new property entrance or upgrading an existing one, Colls Rural Contractors can provide a practical solution designed around your property's layout, access requirements and rural environment.
