Good to Know

Common Questions

What vehicles do you fit out?

We work on most popular vans including VW, Toyota HiAce, Ford Transit, Mercedes Sprinter and Renault Master. Contact us to confirm your vehicle.

How long does a fit-out take?

It depends on the scope. Smaller packages can be done in days; full conversions take several weeks. We'll give you a timeline with your quote.

Can I see examples of your work?

Yes — our hire campers are built in-house, and our gallery shows recent projects. You're welcome to visit the workshop by appointment.

How much does a fit-out cost?

Every build is different. Prices vary by vehicle and specification, so we provide a tailored quote after consultation.
