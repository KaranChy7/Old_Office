Good to Know

Common Questions

Do I need tools or experience?

Basic hand tools and a bit of patience are all you need. Instructions are step by step, and we're a phone call away.

Will the kit fit my vehicle?

Kits are sized to specific vehicles. Tell us your make, model and year and we'll confirm the fit.

Can you install it for me?

Yes — if you'd rather not DIY, we offer an optional fitting service at our Arundel workshop.
