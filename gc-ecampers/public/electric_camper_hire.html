Good to Know

Common Questions

Do I need a special licence to drive the ID. Buzz?

No. A standard full car licence is all you need. The Buzz drives like a regular car and we walk you through everything at pickup.

How far can I travel on a charge?

Up to around 420km depending on speed, terrain and load. We map fast chargers along your route so range is never a worry.

Is charging included in the hire?

We supply the charging cables and PlugShare access. Public charging costs during your trip are paid as you go.

Can I bring my dog?

Get in touch before booking — we can advise on pet-friendly options for your dates.
