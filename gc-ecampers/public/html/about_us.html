Our Story

Built on the
Gold Coast

GC-E Campers was born from a simple idea: that road tripping should be as exciting as the destination. We started with a single van, a workshop in Arundel, and a passion for thoughtful design.

Today we're the Gold Coast's only dedicated electric and hybrid camper hire, and a go-to workshop for bespoke custom fit-outs that transform everyday vans into adventure machines.

Everything is hand-crafted locally. Every hire camper is maintained to the highest standard. And every fit-out is built like we're building it for ourselves.
