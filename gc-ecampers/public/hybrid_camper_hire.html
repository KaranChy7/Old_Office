Good to Know

Common Questions

How many people does the Mini sleep?

Two adults comfortably in the roof-top tent.

Is the roof-top tent hard to set up?

Not at all. It opens in a few minutes and we demonstrate it at pickup so you're confident before you leave.

Can I take it on dirt roads?

Well-maintained gravel roads to campgrounds are fine. Let us know your plans and we'll advise on your route.
