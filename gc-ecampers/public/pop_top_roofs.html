Good to Know

Common Questions

Will a pop-top fit my van?

Pop-tops suit many popular vans. Send us your make, model and year and we'll confirm the options.

Is it waterproof?

Yes. Roofs are properly sealed and water tested before handover. Canvas should be dried out after wet trips to keep it in top condition.

Can I add a pop-top as part of a full fit-out?

Absolutely — it's often best to plan the roof alongside your interior layout. See our Custom Fit-Outs service.
