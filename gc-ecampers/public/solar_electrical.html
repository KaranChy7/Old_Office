Good to Know

Common Questions

How big a battery do I need?

It depends on your fridge, devices and how long you stay off-grid. We do a quick usage assessment and recommend the right size.

Can you add solar to my existing camper?

Yes. We can upgrade existing systems or add solar, lithium and DC-DC charging to vans and campers already on the road.

Can I run 240V appliances?

With a correctly sized inverter and battery, yes. We'll advise on what's realistic for your setup.
