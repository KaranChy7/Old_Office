Garden Maintenance

A garden needs regular care to stay healthy and looking its best. Our garden maintenance service takes that work off your hands. We weed garden beds, trim hedges and shrubs, prune plants, top up mulch and tidy edges, so your garden always looks neat and well looked after. With 12 years of experience, we know what plants need at each time of year and we tailor every visit to suit your garden.

We look after homes, rental properties, townhouses and commercial sites across Kellyville and the Hills District. Whether you need a one-off tidy-up or regular weekly, fortnightly or monthly visits, we work to a schedule that suits you and your budget.

Regular maintenance stops weeds taking over, keeps hedges in shape and helps plants grow strong and healthy. Small problems are dealt with early, before they turn into a bigger and more costly job. Your garden stays tidy all year round instead of only after a big clean-up.

Every visit finishes with a proper clean-up. We sweep paths and driveways, blow down hard surfaces and take all the clippings and green waste away with us, so all that is left is a fresh, tidy garden you can enjoy.

Landscaping & Gardening

Our landscaping and gardening service helps you turn a plain or tired yard into an outdoor space you love. We plan and build new garden beds, plant shrubs, flowers and hedges, lay fresh mulch and turf, and shape the layout of your yard so it looks great and is easy to look after.

We start by talking with you about how you use your yard, the look you want and your budget. From there we suggest plants and ideas that suit the space, the soil and the Sydney climate, so your new garden settles in well and keeps looking good for years.

Whether it is a small front garden refresh, new planting along a fence line or a full backyard makeover, we handle the job from start to finish. We prepare the ground, remove old plants and weeds, improve the soil and put everything in place neatly and carefully.

Good landscaping adds street appeal and value to your property. Once the work is done, we can also look after your new garden with regular maintenance visits, so it stays healthy and well presented.

Rubbish Removal

Piles of green waste, old branches and general rubbish can quickly take over a yard. Our rubbish removal service clears it all away for you. We load, remove and dispose of garden waste, tree and hedge cuttings, leaves, old pots, soil and general household rubbish from your yard, so you do not have to hire a skip or make trips to the tip.

We are often booked for garden clean-ups, end of lease clean-ups, pre-sale tidy-ups and after storms. Tell us what needs to go and we will give you a clear, upfront price before we start.

We do all the heavy lifting and leave the area swept and tidy when we are finished. Waste is disposed of responsibly, and green waste is recycled wherever we can.

Rubbish removal can be booked on its own or added to a lawn mowing, garden maintenance or landscaping job, so your whole property is cleared and looking its best in one visit.

Lawn Mowing

A freshly mowed lawn makes the whole property look better. Our lawn mowing service keeps your grass cut to the right height, with neat edges along paths, driveways and garden beds. We use well-maintained mowers, line trimmers and edgers to give your lawn a clean, even, professional finish.

We mow lawns of all sizes, from small front yards to large blocks and commercial grounds. You can book us for a one-off mow or a regular schedule, and we will adjust the timing to suit the season and how fast your grass is growing.

Cutting at the right height and on a regular schedule helps your lawn grow thicker and greener, keeps weeds down and helps it handle the hot Sydney summers. If your lawn is badly overgrown, we can bring it back under control and then keep it that way.

After every mow we blow clippings off paths, driveways and patios and take the green waste away, so your yard is left neat and ready to enjoy.

Native Green Lawn And Garden Services is a Kellyville based lawn and garden business with 12 years of experience. We look after lawns, gardens and green waste for homes and businesses across the Hills District and greater Sydney.
