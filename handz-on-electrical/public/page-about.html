About Hortsol Pty Ltd

Local lawn, garden & property care, led by Cameron Lostroh

Hortsol is a lawn, garden and property maintenance business based in Haigslea, Queensland. We give local home and acreage owners a dependable, organised outdoor maintenance service.

From regular mowing on a suburban block to keeping acreage under control, we look after the whole outdoor space. Every job is planned around the property and carried out with care, so your lawns, gardens and grounds stay tidy and healthy all year round.

  • Lawn & acreage mowing
  • Garden care & landscaping
  • Weed management & spraying
  • Turf installation & lawn renovation
