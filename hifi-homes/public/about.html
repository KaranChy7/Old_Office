Our Story

Who We Are

Clyde North-based. Trade-trusted. Fixtures we stand behind.

Hifi Homes supplies bathroom, kitchen and laundry fixtures to builders, renovators and homeowners across Melbourne. From vanities and basins to tapware, showers and hardware, every order gets the same attention — accurate stock, honest advice, and pricing that doesn't move once it's quoted.

Whether it's a single vanity for a renovation or the full fit-out for a new build, every order is handled with the same trade-standard care — bathroom, kitchen, laundry or accessories. We supply builders, renovators and homeowners across Clyde North and greater Melbourne, including Casey, Cardinia and the South East.

Our Journey

From First Order To A Trusted Supplier

The Beginning

A Small Range, Done Properly

Hifi Homes starts out supplying bathroom basics to local renovators — building a reputation the only way that lasts: accurate stock and straight answers, every time.

Growing The Range

Kitchen & Laundry Added

As demand grows, the range expands beyond the bathroom — kitchen sinks, mixers and laundry tubs brought in-house to cover more of every project.

Going Professional

Trade Accounts, Trade Pricing

Hifi Homes formalises trade pricing and accounts, putting hands-on product knowledge on a proper professional footing for builders and renovators.

Today

A Growing Catalogue

Hifi Homes now supplies clients across Melbourne — same range discipline, same honest pricing, on every order, big or small.
