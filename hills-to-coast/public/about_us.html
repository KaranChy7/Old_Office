our story

"Qualified. Professional. Perth's Trusted Painter."

True Tee Finishes is a Perth-based painting and decorating business providing professional painting services across residential, commercial and maintenance work. I am a qualified painter and decorator with a Certificate III in Painting and Decorating and an EWP ticket.

I focus heavily on preparation, quality finishes and leaving every job clean and tidy. Travel for quotes can be arranged throughout the Perth metro area depending on location.

Every project is approached with care, clear communication and close attention to detail from start to finish. I take the time to properly prepare each surface so the final result looks professional, lasts longer and meets the expectations of each client.

Whether the job involves a fresh new look, ongoing maintenance or restoring an existing space, I aim to provide dependable workmanship and a smooth experience throughout the project. My goal is to complete every job neatly, efficiently and to a standard I can be proud of.
