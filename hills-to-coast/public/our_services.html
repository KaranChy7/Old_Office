Interior Painting

True Tee Finishes specialises in interior painting for homes and businesses across Perth, covering walls, ceilings, doors, frames, and trims with clean lines and a smooth, professional finish.

Every job starts with proper surface preparation—patching, sanding, and priming—so the paint goes on evenly and holds up over time, rather than just covering up what's underneath.

Whether it's a single room refresh or a full interior repaint, careful masking and tidy site management mean your home or workplace stays protected and presentable throughout the job. The right colour and finish are recommended for each surface, so the result looks intentional and lasts.

Good preparation is the difference between a paint job that looks great for a few months and one that holds its finish for years—cracks, stains, and old flaking paint are addressed before any new coat is applied, not painted over.

Exterior Painting

Exterior painting protects your property from Perth's weather while lifting its street appeal, covering weatherboards, render, fascias, and trims with a durable, even finish.

Surfaces are properly prepared before painting—washing, scraping, sanding, and priming as needed—so the new coat adheres properly and resists cracking, peeling, and fading.

From full exterior repaints to targeted maintenance painting on eaves, gutters, and trims, the job is scheduled around the weather and completed with clear communication from start to finish.

A well-prepared and well-painted exterior does more than look good on the day—it's your property's first line of defence against sun, rain, and general wear, so getting the preparation right matters just as much as the paint itself.

Residential Painting

Whether you're renovating or simply refreshing a tired home, residential painting services cover everything from colour selection through to a clean, precise finish tailored to your household.

Living areas, bedrooms, kitchens, and hallways all get the same careful preparation and attention to detail, so the finished result looks consistent from room to room.

Furniture-friendly scheduling and tidy site management keep the process as stress-free as possible, so your home stays livable while the work is done.

A well-chosen colour scheme and a properly applied finish don't just look good on completion day—they add genuine value to your home and hold up to years of everyday family life.

Commercial Painting

Commercial painting services for offices, retail spaces, and other business premises balance appearance with the practical demands of a working environment.

Work is scheduled around your operating hours, minimising disruption to staff and customers while still delivering a durable, professional finish.

From a single office refresh to a full commercial repaint, clear communication and efficient project management keep the job on schedule from initial quote to final coat.

Property managers and business owners can plan painting work around lease timelines and shutdown periods, keeping disruption to staff and customers to a minimum while the space is brought up to standard.

Feature Walls

A well-executed feature wall adds character to any room, and getting sharp, straight lines is where careful preparation and a steady hand really show.

Colour, finish, and even bold techniques are discussed up front, so the result matches the look you have in mind for the space.

Feature walls are finished to the same standard as the rest of the room, with clean edges against skirting, cornices, and adjoining walls.

A feature wall is one of the most cost-effective ways to transform a room, and a crisp, properly finished result is what separates a professional job from a DIY attempt.

Spray Painting

Spray painting delivers a smooth, even finish across larger surfaces, doors, and trims, often faster and more consistently than a brush or roller alone.

Careful masking and site protection keep overspray away from surfaces that aren't being painted, so the rest of your home or workplace stays clean throughout the job.

Spray application suits everything from feature walls and ceilings to doors, frames, and cabinetry, giving a factory-smooth result on the surfaces that need it.

Getting spray painting right comes down to preparation and technique—correct thinning, even passes, and the right equipment for the surface are what produce a clean, professional finish.

Maintenance Painting & Repaints

Maintenance painting keeps a property looking its best year after year, addressing scuffs, fading, and wear before they turn into bigger problems.

Repaints refresh tired interiors and exteriors alike, matching existing colours or helping you choose a new scheme where you're ready for a change.

Regular maintenance painting is often far more cost-effective than letting a property go too long between coats, protecting surfaces and keeping presentation consistent.

Landlords, property managers, and homeowners alike benefit from a planned maintenance painting schedule that keeps a property presentable and protected without unnecessary expense.

Renovations & New Work

Renovation and new work painting covers freshly built or newly renovated spaces, from bare plaster and new fit-outs through to a fully finished, ready-to-use room.

Coordinating with builders and other trades keeps the painting stage moving smoothly as part of a wider renovation or new build project.

New surfaces are primed and sealed correctly before finishing coats are applied, giving a durable result that's built to last from day one.

Getting the painting stage right on a renovation or new build sets the tone for the whole space, so proper sealing and priming of new surfaces is never skipped.

Ceilings & Walls

Ceilings and walls are painted with careful attention to coverage and finish, correcting minor imperfections along the way for a smooth, even result.

Water stains, hairline cracks, and old patchy paintwork are addressed before painting begins, so the finished ceiling or wall looks genuinely fresh rather than just recoated.

The right sheen and colour are recommended for each room, balancing appearance with practicality for high-traffic areas like hallways and living spaces.

A freshly painted ceiling and set of walls can transform how a room feels, and getting an even, streak-free finish comes down to proper preparation and the right technique.

Doors, Frames, Skirting Boards & Trims

Doors, frames, skirting boards, and trims finish a paint job properly, and getting a clean, durable coat on these high-touch surfaces makes a real difference to the overall result.

These surfaces take more knocks and wear than walls and ceilings, so a harder-wearing finish is used and applied with careful attention to sharp, clean lines.

Whether it's a full set of doors and skirting or just trims around a single renovated area, colours and finishes are matched so the new work blends in with the rest of the home.

Well-painted trims and doors protect the timber underneath from moisture and general wear, extending the life of the woodwork as well as the finish itself.

Surface Preparation, Patching & Repairs

A great paint job starts with what's underneath it, and proper surface preparation ensures every wall, ceiling, and surface is sound and ready for painting.

Cracks, holes, water damage, and flaking paint are checked for and addressed before painting begins, rather than letting problems show through the new coat.

Patching compounds, sanding, and priming are all part of the process used to get a surface genuinely ready for the finish going on top—this step matters just as much on renovations as it does on maintenance jobs.

Skipping proper surface preparation is one of the most common causes of paint failure down the track, and it's far cheaper to fix at this stage than after the new coat is down.

General Painting & Decorating Services

Beyond the core painting work, general painting and decorating services cover the smaller jobs that finish a project properly—touch-ups, colour consultations, and other decorating tasks.

No job is too small, and every task is approached with the same attention to detail as a full repaint, so the finished result blends in seamlessly.

Free quotes are available across the Perth metro area, with honest advice on the right approach for your space and budget.

A professionally completed job, clean and tidy when finished, is the standard held on every piece of work taken on—whether it's a single touch-up or a full decorating project.
