About Us

Lone Wolf Contracting Pty Ltd started with one simple goal: to make outdoor cleaning and garden care easy for busy people. Based in Narellan Vale, we have spent 5 years helping families, landlords and local businesses across South West Sydney look after the outside of their properties.

Hard-Working Team, Honest Approach

We are a small, hands-on crew who take pride in our work. You get the same people from the first call to the final rinse, so nothing gets lost along the way.

From a quick driveway wash to a full garden makeover, we treat every property as if it were our own.
