About ACB

AC BUILT GROUP is a NSW-based bespoke carpentry and construction business built on a commitment to craftsmanship, integrity, and attention to detail. With over a decade of hands-on industry experience, we specialise in delivering high-quality custom carpentry and tailored construction solutions designed to enhance the aesthetic appeal, comfort, and long-term value of every home. We pride ourselves on delivering unique, affordable, and expertly finished results tailored to each client’s individual vision and lifestyle needs. What began as a hobby evolved into a genuine passion—and has grown into a dedicated career—focused on crafting and creating custom solutions for our clients with precision and care while ensuring lasting quality, durability and exceptional client satisfaction in every project.

“WHERE BESPOKE MEETS DETAIL. CRAFTED WITH CARE. BUILT TO LAST.”

At AC BUILT GROUP every project is treated as if it were our own. From concept to completion we take the time to understand how you live, what you need, and what will work best for your space, then bring it to life through thoughtful planning, clear communication and meticulous craftsmanship.

AC BUILT GROUP is fully qualified, licensed, and insured, ensuring every project complies with Australian Standards and industry best practices while being delivered with the highest level of craftsmanship and care—no shortcuts, no compromises, just reliable results you can trust for years to come.
