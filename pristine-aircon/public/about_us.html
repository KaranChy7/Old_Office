About Us

about us

Melbourne Metalwork Specialists

IR Iron Door is a Campbellfield metal fabrication business that makes and installs metal doors, gates, fences and custom metalwork for homes and businesses. Every job is measured on site, built to suit the opening and finished to handle Victorian weather.

Our highly experienced team works with steel and wrought iron every day, so you get solid construction, neat welds and hardware that operates smoothly for years.

We started IR Iron Door to give Melbourne property owners straight answers and a product that is built properly. Whether it is one security door or a full run of commercial fencing, every project gets the same care and attention to detail.

Homeowners come to us for wrought iron entry doors, security screen doors, driveway gates, front fences, balustrades and handrails. Businesses rely on us for steel doors, perimeter fencing, sliding site gates, security grilles and structural steelwork for factories, warehouses, shops and industrial sites.

We work within 100km of Campbellfield, covering Craigieburn, Epping, Broadmeadows, Coburg, Preston, Sunbury, Melton, Werribee, the Melbourne CBD, Dandenong, Frankston and Geelong. Our team is available Mon - Fri, 8am to 5pm, and closed on weekends. Call us to talk about your project and book a free measure and quote.
