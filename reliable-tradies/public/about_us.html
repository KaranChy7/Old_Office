Our Story

20+ Years of Trusted Trade Craft

Reliable Tradies Group was founded on a straightforward belief: South Australians deserve tradespeople who show up on time, do the job right, and treat every property with respect. That principle has guided every project we've taken on since day one.

Based in Salisbury SA, we've spent over a decade building lasting relationships with homeowners, property investors, and commercial clients across the greater Adelaide region. From a single room repaint to a complete property renovation — we bring the same standard of excellence to every job.

Our team of skilled trade specialists covers Painting, Tiling, Flooring, and Concrete — four disciplines under one reliable roof. That means less coordination hassle for you, and a more consistent finish across your entire project.
