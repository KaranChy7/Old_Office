What areas do you service?

We are based in Salisbury SA 5108 and service a SA. This covers most of greater Adelaide and surrounding regions. If you're unsure whether we reach your area, just give us a call and we'll confirm.

Do you provide free quotes?

Yes, absolutely. All quotes are completely free and obligation-free. We'll visit your site, assess the project requirements, and provide a clear, detailed written quote with no hidden fees.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Reliable Tradies Group is fully licensed and insured for all the services we provide. Our ABN is 41 103 231 058. We carry public liability insurance on every job for full protection.

What services do you offer?

We specialise in four core trade services: Painting (interior & exterior), Tiling (bathroom, kitchen, floor, wall), Flooring (hardwood, laminate, hybrid, vinyl), and Concrete (driveways, slabs, paths, decorative). Visit our Services page for a full breakdown.

What are your working hours?

We work Mon – Sat: 7:00am – 4:00pm. We don't operate on Sundays. If you need to reach us outside these hours, you're welcome to leave a message or send an email and we'll get back to you first thing the next business day.

How long does a typical job take?

Project duration varies depending on scope and service type. A room repaint might take a day; a full concrete driveway may take two to three days. We provide a clear timeline estimate in every quote so you know exactly what to expect.

Do you supply materials or do I need to?

We can supply all necessary materials as part of your quote, or work with materials you've already sourced. We'll discuss this during the quoting process and recommend the best approach for your project and budget.
