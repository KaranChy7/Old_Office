Our Story

Built From the Ground Up

AP Heating & Cooling Systems started the way most good things do — with hard work, a ute full of tools, and a commitment to get it right. Over 8+ years in the trade, the goal has stayed simple: build a business Salisbury South and Adelaide could genuinely rely on.

Back then, it was one technician and a phone that rang more from word-of-mouth than anything else. Eight years later, that phone still rings for the same reason.

Today AP handles everything from a single split system repair to full industrial installation contracts. Our values haven't shifted: show up on time, communicate honestly, and produce work that holds up to inspection.

"We didn't grow by cutting corners. We grew by being the most thorough."
