Cooling Services

Cooling Systems

Stay Cool, All Summer Long

When the Adelaide heat sets in, you need a cooling system that actually keeps up. At AP Heating & Cooling Systems, we supply, install and service every type of air conditioning system — matched to your space, your budget and how you actually use the room.

With 8+ years of experience across Salisbury South and greater Adelaide, our licensed technicians handle everything from a single split system in a bedroom to a fully ducted, zoned solution for the whole home — installed cleanly and backed by manufacturer warranty.

Split Systems

A single indoor and outdoor unit, sized and positioned to suit the room. Our split system installs are quiet, efficient, and finished with a clean cable and pipe run — no shortcuts, no exposed mess.

  • Single & multi-room options
  • Energy-efficient inverter models
  • Quiet indoor operation
  • Clean, tidy pipework
  • Backed by manufacturer warranty

02

Ducted Reverse Cycle

Whole-home comfort delivered through a single, zoned system — heating in winter, cooling in summer, controlled room by room. We design the duct layout around your home, not the other way around.

  • Zoned heating & cooling
  • Concealed ceiling ductwork
  • Central control panel
  • Even temperature throughout
  • Ideal for new builds & renovations

03

Multi Head Split Systems

One outdoor condenser, several indoor heads — cool or heat multiple rooms independently, without the cost or disruption of a full ducted system.

  • One outdoor unit, multiple rooms
  • Independent room control
  • Lower install footprint
  • Cost-effective vs. multiple systems
  • Flexible for home extensions

Cassette Air Conditioners

Ceiling-recessed cassette units deliver even airflow in four directions while staying out of sight — a popular choice for offices, shops and open-plan living areas.

  • Discreet ceiling-mounted design
  • 360° even airflow
  • Suited to commercial fit-outs
  • Slimline, unobtrusive finish
  • Quiet operation

Evaporative Air Conditioners

A cost-effective, energy-efficient way to cool a home through Adelaide's dry summers, using natural evaporation instead of refrigerant gas.

  • Low running costs
  • Continuous fresh air supply
  • Environmentally friendly
  • Well suited to the local climate
  • Simple, low-maintenance system
